How It Works

  1. Recruitment 1 — Intake (Form Trigger) Validates applicant details and real PDF format, saves the CV to Google Drive, and logs the candidate to Google Sheets.
  2. Recruitment 2 — AI Screening (Schedule Trigger) Reads new applications, evaluates each CV against job criteria using AI, and sends interview invites or decline emails.
  3. Recruitment 3 — Error Handler (Error Trigger) Captures any workflow failure automatically, logs error details to a sheet, and dispatches instant email alerts.
  4. RAG AI Assistant (Chat Trigger) Retrieves relevant policy chunks from the vector store and generates grounded answers with source citations.
  5. Policy Loader (Startup, Daily 7 AM & Manual Triggers) Syncs Google Docs from Drive, splits text into overlapping chunks, generates Gemini embeddings, and refreshes the knowledge base.
