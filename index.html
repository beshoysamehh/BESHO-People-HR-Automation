How It Works

Five modular n8n workflows orchestrating intake, AI evaluation, RAG sync, and fault monitoring.

  1. Recruitment 1 — Candidate Intake Form Trigger
    Validates candidate fields and authentic PDF headers, prevents duplicate entries, uploads resumes to Google Drive, and logs applicants to Google Sheets.
  2. Recruitment 2 — AI Screening Schedule Trigger
    Polls pending applications, evaluates each CV against role requirements using AI, updates scores, and sends personalized interview invites or decline emails.
  3. Recruitment 3 — Error Handler Error Trigger
    Monitors all workflows in real time, logs execution failures to a dedicated Google Sheet, and dispatches instant alert emails.
  4. RAG Knowledge Assistant Chat Trigger
    Embeds user queries, searches the in-memory vector store for matching policy chunks, and generates grounded responses with source citations.
  5. Policy Loader Startup • 7 AM Daily • Manual
    Fetches policy docs from Google Drive, converts them to plain text, splits chunks with overlap, generates Gemini embeddings, and refreshes the vector store.
